# Vitreva

A multilingual brochure site whose text is run by the business it belongs to. It is also the first site of this kind, so its vocabulary is written to carry over to later brochure and portfolio clients.

## Language

### People

**Owner**:
The person responsible for a business's site, who decides what it says and who may edit it.
_Avoid_: Client, site owner, user, admin

**Employee**:
A person who works for the Owner's business and edits all content, but does not manage people.
_Avoid_: Editor, staff, collaborator

**IT**:
The person who builds and looks after the site, and who restores access when an Owner or Employee can no longer sign in.
_Avoid_: Admin, developer, maintainer

**Login Site**:
The site where Owners and Employees sign in and the Owner manages people. It also publishes the privacy notice, the provider list and how to get help.
_Avoid_: Portal, dashboard, auth site

**Passcode**:
A short-lived code sent to an Owner or Employee by email or text message, whichever they prefer, used to sign in instead of a password.
_Avoid_: Password, PIN, OTP

### Offer

**Foundation**:
The invisible parts every client's site shares: sign-in, publishing steps, language and SEO rules, and checks.
_Avoid_: Engine, template, framework

**Custom Design**:
Everything made for one business alone: layout, sections, art, motion and the shape of its content.
_Avoid_: Theme, skin, template

**Exit Promise**:
IT's commitment to hand a departing client a full working copy of their site.
_Avoid_: Export, offboarding

### Content

**Content Contract**:
The agreement about what an Owner can edit on their site and the shape it must keep. It checks structure, never wording.
_Avoid_: Schema, content model

**Business Facts**:
The facts about the business that appear on many surfaces at once: phone, email, opening hours and service area.
_Avoid_: Config, site settings, contact info

**Public Claim**:
A statement the site makes about the business that a customer may rely on, such as a delay or a qualification. The Owner is responsible for it.
_Avoid_: Promise, guarantee, marketing copy

**Fixed Page**:
A page that is part of the site's structure and exists in every Active Language.
_Avoid_: Static page, service page

**Blog Post**:
An article created by the Owner or an Employee, which may exist in only one language.
_Avoid_: Article, news item, entry

**Active Language**:
A language the Owner has chosen to offer on their site.
_Avoid_: Locale, translation
